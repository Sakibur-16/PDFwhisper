# PDFwhisper 📑✨

**Your PDFs finally WHISPERs (talk) you back.**

Upload any PDF — scanned, textbook, research paper, novel, whatever — and start getting summurized verison of Whole BOOK or Paper and You can talk ask get suggesions or questions from chatbot. Get accurate answers from any page or chapter instantly. No copy-paste. No searching. Just chat.

Built for students, researchers, professionals, and anyone TIRED of [{Ctrl+F}] or Reading Full Book at once

OR need a specific chapters insight or the book summary

Everything Will be done just in minutes



## 🚀 Features

- **Full OCR Support** – Works perfectly with scanned PDFs and images
- **Chapter & Section Aware** – Ask about specific chapters, pages, or sections
- **Smart Chatbot** – Natural conversation, remembers context
- **API Keys Needed** [FLAW of The Project]
- **Fast & Accurate Answers** – Powered by advanced embeddings + LLM
- **Persistent Chat History** – Come back and continue where you left off
- **Clean Modern UI** – Dark mode, [ONLY PC Ready with proper compiler and vs code]
